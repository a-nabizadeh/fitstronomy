Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Fitstronomy handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Who Controls Your Information

Armin Nabizadeh, operating as Fitstronomy, is the controller of information collected through this website. Privacy questions and requests can be sent through the website contact form.

Information Collected

  • Contact information, including name, email address, and optional telephone number.
  • Coaching application details, including goals, availability, training history, and preferred service.
  • Optional body measurements and an acknowledgement that a separate pre-training safety screening is required.
  • Technical information that service providers may record for security and delivery, such as IP address, browser information, and submission time.

Why Information Is Used

Contact and application information is used to answer enquiries, review general coaching suitability, recommend a service, and take steps requested before a coaching agreement.

This website does not collect diagnoses, injury details, medication information, or PAR-Q answers. A separate safety screening must be completed before coaching begins. Do not enter health information in free-text fields on this website.

Form Delivery And International Processing

Forms are delivered using Web3Forms, operated by Web3Creative. Form contents are processed by Web3Forms and forwarded to the coach's email inbox. Web3Forms states that it does not store form submissions, that security logs containing personal information are deleted periodically, currently every two months, and that its servers are in the US-East region. Its operator is based in India.

Submitting a form can therefore involve processing outside the European Economic Area. Web3Forms' public documentation does not currently identify the specific contractual transfer mechanism used for this service. For that reason, the website forms are not used to collect detailed health information. Review the Web3Forms privacy and GDPR information before submitting.

Do not submit medical records, diagnoses beyond what is needed for screening, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Other Service Providers

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and a version-pinned visual library from Unpkg. Those providers may receive ordinary request information such as an IP address and browser headers. The website does not use advertising pixels or analytics.

Retention

  • Contact enquiries are deleted within 90 days after the final response unless the person becomes a client.
  • Applications from people who do not become clients are deleted within 90 days after the application decision or last contact, whichever is later.
  • Application information for clients is kept during the coaching relationship and deleted within two years after it ends, unless a longer period is required to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim.
  • Web3Forms states that server logs which may contain personal information are deleted every two months.

These periods apply to copies controlled by Fitstronomy, including copies in the receiving email inbox. Deletion from service-provider backups may follow the provider's own backup cycle.

Your Rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your information, and may object to certain processing by contacting Fitstronomy through the website.

You may also lodge a complaint with Finland's Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

Cookies And Browser Storage

The website does not currently use analytics, advertising cookies, or marketing cookies. Temporary session storage is used only to manage the introductory animation and is normally cleared when the browsing session ends.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the website, coaching process, or service providers change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.
