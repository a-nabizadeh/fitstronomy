About me

Your coach,
your partner

Hi, I'm Armin. I've been training seriously for 15 years and coaching other people for the last four, with a personal training certificate behind it. Before the gym it was five years of competitive swimming and three years of aikido, long enough to know what a training cycle feels like from the inside, including the parts where it stalls.

My day job is research. I hold a PhD in astrophysics and work as a data scientist. That isn't a fitness qualification and I won't pretend it is, but it's the reason I coach the way I do. I read the studies themselves rather than the summaries, and I build your next block from your logged numbers rather than from how the last session felt.

Whether you're starting from zero or you've been lifting for years, I'll build a program that challenges you, respects your limits, and changes as your numbers come in.
